🧪 Sandbox 2D — how to play

People Playground meets Garry's Mod, in 2D. Spawn ragdolls & props, grab them with the physgun, weld / rope / balloon contraptions, then test with guns, swords, grenades and fire.

🖱️ Mouse

  • Left click — use current tool
  • Right click — tool alt-action (unweld, unfreeze, cancel ghost)
  • Wheel — zoom · while physgunning: rotate held object
  • Middle-drag / WASD / arrows — pan camera

⌨️ Keys

  • 1–0 tools · Q/E/R/T guns · Z/X/C/V melee & thrown
  • Space pause · G slow-mo · F freeze hovered · Del remove hovered
  • R rotate spawn ghost · H help · M extinguisher

🔫 Toolgun highlights

  • Physgun — drag bodies, wheel rotates, right-click freezes
  • Weld / Rope — click two bodies to link, right-click to cut
  • Balloon / Thruster — click a prop to attach flying power
  • Guns fire from your gunship muzzle (bottom of view) — aim with the laser

🩸 Playground highlights

  • Humans bleed, burn, gib and die. Androids leak oil. Zombies chase the living.
  • Red barrels explode. Glass shatters. Grenades & molotovs start fires.
  • Build a turret, weld it to a crate, strap balloons on and rule the lab.
